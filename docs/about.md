# About and contributing

ELIXIR On-Cloud is the ELIXIR Compute Platform's work on federated cloud
services, a [Driver Project][ga4gh-dps] of the [GA4GH][ga4gh]. The community
files below apply to all repositories of the [GitHub
organization][elixir-on-cloud-github] and are kept in one place, so that they
do not drift apart.

- [Contributing](https://github.com/elixir-cloud-aai/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)
- [Code of conduct](https://github.com/elixir-cloud-aai/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
- [Support and contact](https://github.com/elixir-cloud-aai/.github/blob/main/SUPPORT.md)
- [Security policy](https://github.com/elixir-cloud-aai/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md)
- [License](https://github.com/elixir-cloud-aai/.github/blob/main/LICENSE)
- [Discussions][elixir-on-cloud-forum]: questions and ideas
- [Website](https://elixir-on.cloud/): the nodes, funders and news
- [ELIXIR Compute Platform](https://elixir-europe.org/platforms/compute):
  the ELIXIR platform behind the project

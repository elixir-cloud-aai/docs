# About

ELIXIR On-Cloud is the [ELIXIR Compute
Platform](https://elixir-europe.org/platforms/compute)'s work on federated
cloud services, a [Driver Project][ga4gh-dps] of the [GA4GH][ga4gh].

- [Website](https://elixir-on.cloud/): nodes, funders and news
- [Discussions][elixir-on-cloud-forum]: questions, ideas and announcements
- [Contributing](https://github.com/elixir-cloud-aai/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md):
  how to contribute, code of conduct and security policy
- [Contact](https://github.com/elixir-cloud-aai/.github/blob/main/SUPPORT.md):
  support channels and how to reach the maintainers

The contact and contributing guides apply to all repositories of the [GitHub
organization][elixir-on-cloud-github] and are kept in one place, so that they
do not drift apart.

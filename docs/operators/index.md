# Node operators: start here

This section is for admins of ELIXIR Nodes who run services at their Node or
want to join the ELIXIR On-Cloud federation.

--8<-- "includes/scope.md"

## Where to go

- [Onboarding your compute center](onboarding.md): deploy compute and
  storage and register your TES service.
- [Cloud Registry](cloud-registry.md): the settings and policies of the
  deployment's registry.
- [Using LS Login](ls-login.md): connect services to LS Login.
- Deploying the individual services at your Node:
  [TESK](services/tesk.md), [Funnel](services/funnel.md),
  [proTES](services/protes.md), [proWES](services/prowes.md),
  [CWL-WES](services/cwlwes.md), [DRS-Filer](services/drsfiler.md) and
  [TRS-Filer](services/trsfiler.md).
